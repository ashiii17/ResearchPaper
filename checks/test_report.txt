IMPORTANT INTERPRETATION NOTE:
This comparison is conducted for a single urban agglomeration (Bengaluru) only.
Both the GHSL Built Surface and the Esri Sentinel-2 Land Cover products are model-derived
Earth observation classifications with distinct definitions:
  - GHSL represents continuous built-up surface area (building footprints, m2 per cell).
  - Esri Land Cover Class 7 represents categorical Built Area (parcels, roads, structures).
Consequently, cross-comparison serves strictly as a consistency and alignment check,
and NOT as definitive ground-truth proof of cartographic accuracy.
