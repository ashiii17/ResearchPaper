1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & OBJECTIVES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This report documents the Time Series Analysis (TSA) coursework module for the
India-wide satellite urban-change research project. The module models the temporal
evolution of built-up land cover across India using annual Esri 10 m land-cover
stacks (2017–2025) paired with Sentinel-2 Surface Reflectance imagery.

Key Findings from Pilot Study:
1. Data Integrity: All 5 pilot chips have 9 annual bands matching 'esri_2017' through
   'esri_2025' with 100% valid land pixels (250,000 px / chip / yr, 0 NoData).
2. Non-Stationarity: Augmented Dickey-Fuller (ADF) and KPSS tests confirm that
   urbanizing chips exhibit unit root non-stationarity due to persistent growth trends.
3. Seasonal Holt-Winters Rejection: Holt-Winters seasonal modeling is methodologically
   rejected because annual data has period m=1 (no sub-annual seasonality) and N=9
   observations cannot support seasonal parameter estimation.
4. Forecasting Performance: In a chronological holdout evaluation (Train: 2017–2023,
   Test: 2024–2025), Holt's Linear Exponential Smoothing achieved the lowest average
   error (MAE = 0.4317%, RMSE = 0.4943%), outperforming Naive Persistence (MAE = 0.6758%).
5. MAPE Mathematical Caveat: Mean Absolute Percentage Error (MAPE) is mathematically
   undefined or pathological when actual built fraction is near 0% (e.g. Desert chip);
   MAE and RMSE must serve as the primary evaluation criteria.

5. STEP 4: STATIONARITY ANALYSIS & MODEL JUSTIFICATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Statistical Hypotheses:
• Augmented Dickey-Fuller (ADF):
    - H0: Series has a unit root (Non-stationary)
    - H1: Series is stationary
    - Decision: p < 0.05 rejects H0 in favor of stationarity.
• Kwiatkowski-Phillips-Schmidt-Shin (KPSS):
    - H0: Series is trend/level stationary
    - H1: Series has a unit root (Non-stationary)
    - Decision: p < 0.05 rejects H0 in favor of non-stationarity.

Pilot Results Summary (`tsa/results/stationarity_results.csv`):
- Urban Growth Chips (46R_r101_c74, 44Q_r113_c70, 43R_r130_c111):
    ADF fails to reject unit root (p > 0.90), while KPSS rejects stationarity (p < 0.05).
    Conclusion: Unit Root / Non-Stationary. First differencing (d=1) is mathematically required.
- Urban Stable (44R_r131_c81) & Desert (43R_r81_c35):
    Both ADF and KPSS fail to reject.
    Conclusion: Inconclusive due to small sample size (N=9) and low asymptotic test power.

Model Formulation & Justification:
1. Naive Persistence Baseline: y_{T+h} = y_T. Non-parametric reference.
2. Drift Baseline: y_{T+h} = y_T + h * [(y_T - y_1) / (T - 1)]. Captures linear secular trend.
3. Constrained ARIMA: ARIMA(0,1,0) with drift. Random walk on first differences.
4. Holt's Linear Exponential Smoothing: Level (alpha) + Trend (beta).
5. Seasonal Holt-Winters Rejection: Rejected because annual data has period m=1.


6. STEP 5: CHRONOLOGICAL HOLDOUT FORECAST EVALUATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Protocol:
- Train Split: 2017–2023 (N=7 annual observations).
- Test Split: 2024–2025 (H=2 annual observations).
- Zero Temporal Shuffling: Preserves strict causality.
- Output: `tsa/results/forecast_evaluation.csv`.

Summary of Model Error Metrics (Mean across Pilot Chips):
| Model | Mean MAE (%) | Mean RMSE (%) | Strengths / Weaknesses |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Holt's Linear Trend** | **0.4317%** | **0.4943%** | **Best Overall**: Adapts to local trend momentum |
| **Naive Persistence** | 0.6758% | 0.7588% | Reliable on stable chips; lags behind rapid growth |
| **Drift Baseline** | 0.6896% | 0.7704% | Over-projects when historical growth temporarily plateaus |
| **ARIMA(0,1,0) with Drift** | 0.6896% | 0.7704% | Mathematically coincides with drift on N=7 series |

MAPE Exclusion Rule:
On Chip 43R_r81_c35 (Desert), actual built fraction in 2025 is 0.0084%. Computing MAPE
produces division by near-zero resulting in artificial error distortion (> 7,000%).
MAPE is strictly excluded when actual value is < 0.05%; MAE and RMSE are the primary metrics.


7. METHODOLOGICAL LIMITATIONS & NEXT STEPS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Sample Size Limitation: Nine annual observations (2017–2025) represent a very short
   time series, constraining complex lag selection and high-order ARIMA estimation.
2. Pseudo-Label Noise: Annual Esri labels are deep-learning predictions (Microsoft AI
   for Earth), introducing sensor and phenological variance between years.
3. Scaling to 1,500 Chips: The complete dataset can be processed using `build_timeseries.py`
   to compute nationwide urbanization trends across all 17 UTM zones.
